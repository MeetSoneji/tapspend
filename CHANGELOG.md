# Changelog

## 1.1.0
- Auto-capture: reads bank/card SMS and UPI app notifications on the device and lists each payment for a one-tap OK.
- Review inbox, review notifications with Save / Change / Not spending buttons, and a daily reminder at a time you choose.
- Credit card bill payments, self-transfers, wallet top-ups, investments and ATM withdrawals are recognised as not spending.
- Refunds and cashback lower spend; add silent refunds with "Mark refunded".
- Imports the last 90 days of bank alerts on first run.
- New design: paper and ink, one green for "confirmed", Mukta typeface, no gradients.

## 1.0.1
- Removed the app lock. Updating from 1.0.0 unlocks the app; no data is lost.
- Removed the monthly budget; the app focuses on tracking what you spend.

## 1.0.0
First release: Quick Log sheet, home-screen widget, Quick Settings tile, app shortcut, Home / History / Insights / Settings, custom categories, multi-currency, budget ring, CSV export, encrypted backup & restore, app lock, a futuristic dark-first design.
