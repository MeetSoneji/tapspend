Tapspend reads the bank and card alerts already arriving on your phone, works out what each payment was, and puts it in an inbox for a one-tap OK.

<b>Catches what you forget</b>
• Reads bank and card SMS and UPI app notifications on the device.
• Suggests a category for each payment and learns from your changes.
• Confirm from the notification itself, or later in the inbox. A daily reminder at a time you choose.
• Imports the last 90 days of alerts on first run.

<b>Understands credit cards</b>
• A swipe is the spend; paying the card bill is not counted again.
• Self-transfers, wallet top-ups, investments and ATM withdrawals are not spending.
• Refunds and cashback lower your spend. Add silent refunds in one tap.

<b>Private</b>
• No internet permission at all. Message text is never stored.
• No accounts, ads, analytics or trackers.

Free and open source (Apache-2.0).
