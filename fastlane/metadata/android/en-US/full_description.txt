Log it in a flash. Know where it went.

Tapspend is a tiny, private expense tracker built around one promise: log an expense in under 3 seconds, without even opening the app.

<b>Fast</b>
• Home-screen widget: tap +, type the amount, tap a category. Done.
• Quick Settings tile and a long-press app shortcut for the same sheet.
• Big custom keypad with haptics, Indian number grouping (₹1,00,000) and any currency.

<b>Private by design</b>
• No INTERNET permission at all. It cannot send your data anywhere, and you can verify that in the manifest.
• No accounts, no ads, no analytics, no trackers.
• Hide amounts on the widget and hide the app from screenshots and Recents.

<b>Useful</b>
• Daily and category insights, search and filters.
• Salary-cycle months (start the month on any day 1 to 28).
• Export to CSV, or a password-encrypted backup you can restore on a new phone.

<b>Gentle on your battery</b>
• No background services, no polling. The widget refreshes only when your data changes, plus one tiny alarm at midnight.

Free and open source (Apache-2.0).
